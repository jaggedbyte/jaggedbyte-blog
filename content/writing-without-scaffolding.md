Drafts get better when you delete the sentence that explains what
the next sentence is about to do. Readers don't need a table of
contents for a paragraph.

Cut the throat-clearing. Cut "in this post I will." Start with the
thing itself and trust that the shape of it will be clear without
a sign pointing at it first.
