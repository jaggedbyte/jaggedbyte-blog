Most interfaces fail the same way: every element wants to be the
important one, so nothing is. The fix isn't more hierarchy, it's less
color.

Pick one accent. Let it mean something — a link, an action, a single
signal — and leave everything else in gray. The eye finds it
immediately precisely because it's the only thing asking to be found.
