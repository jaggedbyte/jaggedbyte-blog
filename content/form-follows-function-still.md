Every few years someone rediscovers Dieter Rams and writes the same
piece about restraint. This is that piece, except shorter.

The ten principles hold up not because they were clever, but because
they were obvious and nobody wanted to follow them. Good design is
as little design as possible — not zero, just little. The discipline
is in knowing what to cut.

A dashboard with six numbers on it, floating in dark space with no
frame around any of them, does more than a dashboard with borders
around every panel. Nothing hidden, nothing extra.
